Emergent Mind Panoramas — in VR

A gaze/point gallery of the public Emergent Mind 360° panoramas, viewable inside your headset.

On the Quest: tap Start, then press the goggles / “Enter VR” icon at the bottom‑right. Point a controller at a thumbnail and pull the trigger. Use the bar at the bottom of your view to go Prev / Next or back to the Gallery.

On a computer: click a thumbnail; drag to look around; M gallery, ←/→ prev/next.
