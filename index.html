Emergent Mind Panoramas — in VR

A gaze/point gallery of the public Emergent Mind 360° panoramas, viewable inside your headset.

On the Quest: tap Start, then press the goggles / “Enter VR” icon at the bottom‑right. Point a controller at a thumbnail and pull the trigger to open it. Controls: X = next, Y = prev, A/B = back to gallery, left grip = exit VR. Hold the right grip anytime to show these controls in-headset.

On a computer: click a thumbnail; drag to look around; M gallery, ←/→ prev/next, H toggle controls.
