Emergent Mind Panoramas — in VR

A gaze/point gallery of the public Emergent Mind 360° panoramas, viewable inside your headset. The three big collections (World History, Space, Artistic Styles) appear as folders at the front.

On the Quest: tap Start, then press the goggles / “Enter VR” icon at the bottom‑right. Point a controller at a folder or panorama and pull the trigger. X/Y = next/prev panorama (or page through a collection), A/B = back, left grip = exit VR. Hold the right grip anytime to show the controls.

On a computer: click to open; drag to look around; M back, ←/→ prev/next or page, H toggle controls.
